Frequently asked questions

Do I need my own API key?
Yes. Curio uses your API key for LLM calls (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). You pay the provider directly. Curio never charges for model usage.
Is my code sent to your servers?
The Free tier is fully local — nothing leaves your machine. Pro and Team sync your knowledge graph (lessons, not source code) to encrypted cloud storage.
What does Curio actually learn?
Curio learns compressed lessons — not raw files. "Auth uses JWT RS256, refresh tokens expire in 7 days" — sourced, dated, and confidence-scored. Not the entire config file.
Can I export or delete my knowledge?
Yes. Export your knowledge graph as JSON at any time. Delete everything with one command. You own your data, always.
How is this different from RAG?
RAG retrieves what matches a query. Curio decides what's worth learning, verifies it, stores it as a lesson, and forgets what becomes irrelevant. It's a learning system, not a search system.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
Yes. 14 days, no credit card required. Full access to Pro features. Downgrade to Free anytime.
